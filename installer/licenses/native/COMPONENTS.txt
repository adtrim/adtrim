Native media components
=======================

FFmpeg and libmpv are built from retained sources for this AdTrim release.
The matching source package and build instructions are available alongside the installer at:
https://github.com/adtrim/adtrim/releases/tag/v1.1.0

Archive: AdTrim-Media-Sources-v1.1.0.tar.gz
The archive includes all listed source snapshots, headers and shader build inputs.

FreeType is used under its GPL option. The combined FFmpeg/libmpv binaries are
distributed under GPLv3; individual components retain their upstream notices.
